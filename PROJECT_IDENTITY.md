# Caption Lantern identity

- Mechanism signature: a frozen transcript, caption track, and accessibility standard become one attributable cue audit; one corrected track may be re-audited without erasing the first result.
- Product metaphor: a projection booth. Evidence is mounted as reel elements and findings appear on a scrub-able film strip.
- Narrative voice: an observant projectionist—plain, precise, never theatrical about accessibility.
- Visual grammar: near-black cinema, warm projector light, cobalt cue marks, narrow captions, one panoramic stage.
- Interaction grammar: mount reel -> light reel -> inspect marked cues -> mount one repair.
- Evidence story: every fetched source is digest-bound; the standard and transcript must remain unchanged during repair.
- Documentation form: screening notes, cue legend, and a reproduction call sheet rather than generic feature sections.
